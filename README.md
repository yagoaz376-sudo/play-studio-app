# Play Studio

A browser editor for the TC-Helicon **Play Acoustic** and **Play Electric** over USB. It runs in Chrome or Edge on a PC or Mac, or in Chrome on Android. Open it here: https://yagoaz376-sudo.github.io/play-studio-app/

Built on [open-acoustic](https://github.com/michaelbeyhs/open-acoustic) by michaelbeyhs: the SysEx protocol, the MIDI link and the Play Acoustic menus and pictures in `vendor/` come from that project. The Play Acoustic, the Play Electric, their firmware and their artwork belong to their owners.

How to use it, in Portuguese: [LEIA-ME.md](LEIA-ME.md).
